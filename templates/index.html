AES-256-GCM Encryption

Transforms confidential plaintext into cryptographically strong ciphertext with a 128-bit authentication tag. Keys are derived using PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 with 600,000 iterations and a fresh 32-byte salt per operation.

#️⃣

SHA-256 Integrity Verification

Generates a fixed 256-bit cryptographic digest over all payload components $(salt \parallel nonce \parallel ciphertext)$. Guarantees immediate detection if the stego image is modified or tampered with at the pixel level.

🖼️

Spatial LSB Steganography

Conceals the versioned binary payload directly inside the least significant bits of RGB image channels. Produces imperceptible visual alterations with high Peak Signal-to-Noise Ratio (PSNR) values in lossless PNG.
